About LIRO

LIRO is the next chapter of a proven Canadian success story, bringing elevated design, refined craftsmanship, and uncompromising quality to homes across the United States.

Built on the foundation of LIRO Canada’s success since 2016, LIRO has earned its reputation by delivering luxury plumbing fixtures that merge timeless aesthetics with reliable performance.

Now expanding into the U.S. market, LIRO continues that mission, offering thoughtfully curated collections designed for modern living, architectural spaces, and high-end residential projects.

At LIRO, we believe fixtures are more than functional elements — they define the experience of a space. Every product reflects our commitment to precision, durability, and design excellence, giving homeowners, designers, and builders the confidence to create interiors that are both bold and enduring.
