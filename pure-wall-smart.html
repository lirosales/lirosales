Pure Wall Smart

A wall-hung reinterpretation of our intelligent platform — the Kirin pressure-assist system built into a slim concealed frame, eliminating the tank entirely for a floating, architectural silhouette.

The Pure Wall Smart redefines modern bathroom innovation by combining a sleek wall-hung design with advanced intelligent bidet technology and the revolutionary KIRIN pressure-assist flushing system. Unlike traditional wall-mounted toilets requiring bulky concealed tanks, Pure Wall Smart utilizes an integrated tankless pressure-assisted system, delivering powerful siphonic flushing while maximizing space and simplifying installation.

Designed for luxury residential and commercial applications, it offers superior hygiene, comfort, water efficiency, and contemporary aesthetics.

Tankless Wall-Hung DesignEliminates the need for a concealed water tank, maximizing usable wall space.
KIRIN Pressure-Assist Flushing SystemPowerful siphonic flushing using only 3.8L per flush, without electricity for the flush itself.
