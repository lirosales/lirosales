Pure Pro

Adds feminine & posterior wash at room temperature, an antibacterial self-cleaning nozzle, and remote button flush — Kirin pressure assist throughout, still no electricity for the flush itself.

The Pure Pro introduces a new generation of bathroom technology by combining a sleek tankless one-piece design with the powerful Kirin pressure-assist flushing system. Engineered for modern living, this toilet delivers strong and reliable flushing performance without requiring electricity, making it an efficient and dependable solution for contemporary homes.

Designed with comfort, hygiene, and minimalist aesthetics in mind, Pure Pro features an integrated dual-wash system for feminine and posterior cleaning using room-temperature water. Its fully skirted profile, ADA-compliant universal height, and ultra-efficient water consumption provide the perfect balance between advanced functionality and modern bathroom design.

No Electricity RequiredAdvanced mechanical flushing system delivers powerful performance without electrical components.
Kirin Pressure-Assist Flushing SystemHigh-efficiency pressure-assist technology provides powerful, consistent flushing with minimal water usage.
Remote Button FlushBluetooth-paired remote flush button allows flexible installation anywhere in the bathroom.
Tankless Modern DesignSleek one-piece construction with concealed trapway creates a minimalist, modern aesthetic.
