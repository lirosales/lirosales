Pure Smart

Our flagship intelligent toilet — the full Kirin pressure-assist platform in a fully skirted one-piece body with front & rear wash, warm air drying, auto lid, and smart remote control.

The Pure Smart represents the next evolution of bathroom technology, combining a sleek tankless one-piece design with advanced intelligent bidet functionality and the powerful Kirin pressure-assist flushing system. Designed for modern luxury bathrooms, this intelligent toilet enhances comfort, hygiene, and convenience while maintaining exceptional water efficiency.

Featuring automatic flushing, adjustable wash functions, warm air drying, and smart temperature controls, Pure Smart delivers a fully integrated personal care experience. Its minimalist fully skirted profile, ADA-compliant universal height, and CUPC certification make it an ideal solution for both high-end residential and commercial spaces.
