Pure One Plus

Everything in Pure One, plus wireless remote button flush — for powder rooms and primary suites that want a hands-free finish without going electric.

The Pure One Plus redefines modern bathroom performance with a sleek tankless one-piece design combined with powerful Kirin pressure-assist flushing technology and a wireless remote flush button. Engineered for efficiency, durability, and minimalist aesthetics, this toilet delivers powerful flushing performance without requiring electricity, making it an ideal solution for both residential and commercial spaces.

Its fully skirted profile, ADA-compliant universal height, and ultra-efficient water consumption provide the perfect balance between modern design, comfort, and sustainability.

No Electricity RequiredReliable mechanical flushing system eliminates electrical components while maintaining powerful performance.
Wireless Remote Flush ButtonPre-paired remote button installs anywhere for a clean, minimalist bathroom with effortless control.
Tankless Modern DesignMinimalist one-piece construction with concealed trapway creates a sleek, contemporary aesthetic.
Kirin Pressure-Assist Flushing SystemAdvanced pressure-assist technology delivers strong, consistent flushing with minimal water usage.
Ultra-Efficient Water ConsumptionUses only 3.8 LPF (1.0 GPF) for exceptional water savings without sacrificing performance.
Universal Height ComfortADA compliant height provides enhanced accessibility and comfort.
